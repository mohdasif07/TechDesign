Arqvexa Cover / Banner Sizes
============================

For Google Business Profile cover photo and similar wide banners.

REQUIRED FORMAT: Landscape (about 1.91:1 ratio)

FILES
-----
logo-cover-1200x628.png   Recommended (1200 x 628)
logo-cover-600x314.png    Minimum (600 x 314)
logo-cover-2400x1256.png  High-res (2x recommended)
logo-cover-4096x2144.png  Maximum quality

SOURCE
------
logo-cover-1200x628.svg

REGENERATE
----------
convert -background none public/brand/logo-cover-1200x628.svg -resize 1200x628 public/brand/logo-cover-1200x628.png
convert -background none public/brand/logo-cover-1200x628.svg -resize 600x314 public/brand/logo-cover-600x314.png
convert -background none public/brand/logo-cover-1200x628.svg -resize 2400x1256 public/brand/logo-cover-2400x1256.png
convert -background none public/brand/logo-cover-1200x628.svg -resize 4096x2144 public/brand/logo-cover-4096x2144.png
